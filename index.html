About Me

The short version

The mark in the logo is ऋ — ṛ, said roughly like "ri." You don't need to read it to belong here; consider this the door held open.

Yes, Ritwik is usually a guy’s name. No, I’m not a guy. Yes, I’ve heard the question a hundred times, and yes, I’m still smiling. :)

I was a science kid who dodged engineering, chose colors over circuits, and somehow ended up living in VS Code with an engineer title anyway. The universe has jokes, and so do I.

I’m scared of lizards, water, heights and speed. I’m not scared of a challenge. Fear is just a very loud “this will be good,” which is why I keep saying yes to the things that make my pulse jump. I tell stories for a living, even when the medium is a button.

Off the clock, I’m shouting at F1 cars like they can hear me, cooking well above my skill level, or smashing shuttles across a badminton court. Not the right fit for a role? No hard feelings. But a match on court? Always.
